The Craft

A weekly chocolate ritual

The Weekend Cakes program is one of Kanya's favourite parts of the week. Every Monday a small menu of three or four cakes is announced — drawn from a rotation of sixteen recipes including dark chocolate ganache, white chocolate chiffon, classic appeltert, granadilla and seasonal favourites. The menu lands on the WhatsApp list and on Instagram in the morning. Orders close midday Wednesday. Delivery is the following Friday.

There is no customisation — that is part of the joy. It is a chef's-choice menu, the same way a small restaurant works a Sunday lunch. You choose your favourite from what's offered that week, and Kanya bakes it on Friday. The reward is the variety — over a year, you taste the full range of the kitchen.
