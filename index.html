The Baker Behind the Chocolate

Hello, I'm Kanya

She was four years old when her mother first let her help in the kitchen. She still remembers that first cake — pink, with pink icing.

Two decades later, Kanya Hunt is Cape Town's foremost chocolate cake artist — a master chocolatier who works exclusively with Lindt couverture. Every decorative element you see is chocolate, formed entirely by her hands. The protea petals. The delicate ruffles. The spirals. All chocolate.

Her background in dietetics gives her a rare analytical understanding of what makes flavour work — how layers interact, how richness balances, how texture and temperature create a complete experience. She doesn't just bake. She designs something you taste long after you've eaten it.

"Chocolate is not just an ingredient. It is everything I want to say that words cannot."

✦ "In my spare time I run and cycle — because then I can eat more cake."

What I Create

Every creation, bespoke

Whether a grand wedding centrepiece or a quiet Sunday tart, every cake begins with the same question: what do you want people to feel when they taste it?

Wedding Cakes

Lindt couverture centrepieces sculpted for your day — dramatic, layered, designed to serve as dessert. Every decorative element handcrafted from chocolate.

Kitchen Cakes

Appeltert, rolkoek, granadilla chiffon, melktert, Hertzoggies. Sunday indulgences — buttery, honeyed, chocolate-drenched — made for the people you love most.

Celebration Cakes

Birthdays, anniversaries, milestones — each bespoke in scale, flavour, and form. The occasion tells you what it needs. The chocolate does the rest.

Croquembouche & Minis

Elegant profiterole towers and handcrafted mini cakes — for events, grazing tables, and the most considered gifts.

How it works

The process is simple and personal. Kanya works directly with every client — no brief is too specific, no flavour too ambitious.

01

Tell me your vision

WhatsApp or the form below. Share your occasion, date, guest count, and any flavour or style inspiration. Every enquiry is welcomed personally by Kanya.

02

We design together

Chocolate varieties, flavour profiles, aesthetic direction. This is where your vision and the craft find each other — a dietician's precision meets a chocolatier's eye.

03

Your cake is created

Every element sculpted by hand with Lindt couverture. Collected or delivered fresh on the day — exactly as you imagined it, and better than you expected.

All enquiries welcomed personally. Kanya responds to every message herself — usually within 24 hours. Wedding dates fill quickly; please enquire at least 2–3 months ahead.
