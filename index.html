The Baker Behind the Chocolate

Hello, I'm Kanya

I was four years old when my mother first let me help in the kitchen. I still remember that first cake — pink, with pink icing.

Two decades later, I work exclusively with fine couverture chocolate — sculpting every decorative element entirely by hand. The protea petals. The delicate ruffles. The spirals. Everything you see is chocolate, and everything is made by me.

My background in dietetics gives me a rare analytical understanding of what makes flavour work — how layers interact, how richness balances, how texture and temperature create a complete experience. I don't just bake. I design something you taste long after you've eaten it.

"Chocolate is not just an ingredient. It is everything I want to say that words cannot."

✦ "In my spare time I run and cycle — because then I can eat more cake."

How it works

The process is simple and personal. Kanya works directly with every client — no brief is too specific, no flavour too ambitious.

01 Tell me your vision

WhatsApp or the form below. Share your occasion, date, guest count, and any flavour or style inspiration. Every enquiry is welcomed personally by Kanya.

02 We design together

Chocolate varieties, flavour profiles, aesthetic direction. This is where your vision and the craft find each other — a dietician's precision meets a chocolatier's eye.

03 Your cake is created

Every element sculpted by hand with premium couverture chocolate. Collected or delivered fresh on the day — exactly as you imagined it, and better than you expected.
