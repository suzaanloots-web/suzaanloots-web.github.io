Twenty-five years ago I fell in love with couverture chocolate, and it has never stopped surprising me. For me it is the ultimate creative medium — it has movement, and a quality that no other material in cake design can match.

Contemporary textures and forms inspire me most. I see endless possibilities in combining chocolate with other mediums — wafer paper, fresh and dried flowers, botanical textures, and seasonal fruit.

I am unapologetically obsessed with quality — the ingredients, the process, the finish. I do not compromise on any of it.
