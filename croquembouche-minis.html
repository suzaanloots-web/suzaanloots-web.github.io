The Craft

Small in scale, full in craft

The petite format changes nothing about the standard. Every mini cake and cupcake is finished with the same couverture chocolate techniques that go into the wedding commissions — the same hand-sculpted petals, the same ganache work, the same eye for proportion. The only difference is the serving size.

The croquembouche sits at the other end of the spectrum: one dramatic piece, built from dozens. Choux pastry filled with crème pâtissière, dipped in caramel, finished in couverture, stacked into a tower that breaks apart at the table. A centrepiece that is also a course.

Individually made means individually perfect. There is nowhere to hide — and no need to.
