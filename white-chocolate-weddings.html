The Craft

The white chocolate discipline

White couverture is the harder of the two mediums to master. It is sweeter, softer, more sensitive to temperature — and it leaves nowhere to hide. Every finish, every pearl, every dragee is exposed in the cool light of white-on-white.

What looks effortless on a finished white couverture cake is the result of careful tempering, slow building, and a tolerance for redoing what isn't right. Kanya's white work is luminous because it is patient.

Soft enough to feel like a memory. Sculpted firmly enough to last the day.
